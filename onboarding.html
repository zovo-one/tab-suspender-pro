Tab Suspender Pro puts tabs you haven't touched for a while to sleep. A sleeping tab stays in your tab strip with its title and icon, costs no memory, and one click brings it back where you left it.

Rough estimate for your open tabs 4.2GB
The estimate counts about 110 MB per open tab. Most of them sit idle until you come back to them.
Sleeps after 30 minutes idle by default. You can change that on the next pages.
Stays in the tab strip. Title and icon stay where they are; the page itself is unloaded.
Wakes with one click and returns to the scroll position you left.

These work from the first tab you open. Change them any time at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

AltShiftSSuspend all othersKeeps the current tab, files the rest.
